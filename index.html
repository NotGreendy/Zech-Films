04 — ABOUT

ZECH FILM PRODUCTIONS

Stories first.
Craft always.

Zech Film Productions is an independent filmmaking brand founded by Zech, a filmmaker and cinematography student whose style is based around trailer and teaser-form editing.

Zech has a background in theatre, screen acting and film production, with a focus on visual storytelling and atmospheric filmmaking, particularly within horror, drama and cinematic short-form work.

Training at PQA Studios / LCM Studios and ongoing study at the Northern School of Art support work across concept development, directing, cinematography, editing and post-production.

Alongside filmmaking, the practice includes photography, Adobe Photoshop, visual design and production planning.

Zech has also collaborated with BBC, BFI, Young Producers, FearScreen and The Northern School of Art from 2025 onward, according to his public FilmFreeway profile: https://filmfreeway.com/Zech-Films.
